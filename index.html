what?

Nobody's audio travels. Every browser in the room runs every jammer's instruments, and only what your hands do goes over the wire (notes and knob moves), each stamped with the moment it should sound on a clock the room shares. So everyone hears the same music at the same instant.

Bring real gear too: tell the studio what synth or drum machine you have, and your bandmates hear its closest software twin (or their own, if they have one). There's a lane for live audio a bar or two behind, and everything technical (timing, MIDI routing, clock out, the network) sits behind the gear button in the studio.

Live audio

Works differently. Your instruments travel as notes and land in time for everyone. Real audio can't beat the network, so the band hears it a whole number of bars late, landing on the same chords one loop around. Great over a loop or a chord cycle; not for tight hits with the band. Use headphones.
