JamJam
Jam live in lockstep. Only the notes travel.

Play together,
land together.

Up to four people jam in real time. Every browser runs everyone's instrument from the same shared engine, so instead of streaming audio, JamJam sends what your hands do, stamped with the moment it should sound. Everyone hears the same thing at the same instant.

01A tiny Cloudflare relay introduces the browsers, then gets out of the way: notes go directly from player to player.
02The browsers agree on one clock. Each note is scheduled a little ahead (the lookahead), just past the slowest link, so it lands everywhere at once.
03Bring your own hardware: pick your synth or drum machine, and the others hear a software twin from the same family.
04The audio lane streams a mic or guitar alongside, a set number of bars late, NINJAM style.
