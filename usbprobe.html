Multichannel input check

Does this browser hand MEGAjam every channel of your audio interface, or just two? This page finds out, and checks MIDI too. Nothing leaves your computer.

  1. Plug in the interface first (OP–XY: COM › USB, pick the 8 channel output), then open this page, ideally in Firefox.
  2. Press Start and allow the microphone (it means "audio inputs"). Pick the interface below.
  3. Play something on each track. Every channel that moves its own meter is getting through on its own.
  4. Press Copy report and paste it back to Claude (or Sam). Record 5 s saves a WAV with every channel, to check in a DAW.
